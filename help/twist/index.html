Katalumo Twist help

Rules, tips and answers for Katalumo Twist.

How to play

Every tower is built of tiles in layers. A tile is free when no other tile lies on it, even partly. Covered tiles are shaded.

Tap a free tile, then tap its twin: the same tile, turned another way. Each tap turns a free tile a quarter. When the twin lies exactly like the first tile, the pair goes. Tap the first tile again to choose another one.

Every tower can be cleared. Take it apart with care: a pair taken too early can bury the last twin of another tile.

Pairs or triples

Levels and Tower of the day use pairs. In Quick tower and on the Endless map you choose: tiles go two at a time or three at a time. With triples, find two twins of the first tile and turn both until all three lie the same way.

Attempts

When no free pair is left, ♻ Shuffle mixes the tiles that are left and uses one attempt. The tiles you have cleared stay cleared. You can also shuffle any time.

When the attempts run out, the tower locks. You can watch an ad or use Tokens for one more attempt, take back moves with Undo, or start again for free: ↺ Restart level or ↺ Restart tower.

Levels 1 to 4 never lock: you get a free attempt to get you started. When no order of moves can clear the tiles that are left, the game takes back the moves that led there for free.

Hints

💡 Hint shows a pair you can take off safely. Every tower has a few free hints. After that a hint costs Tokens, or you can watch an ad for a few more.

Levels and stars

Play the levels in order. Every level is the same for everyone. Every 5th level is Hard, every 25th Very hard: bigger and taller.

The faster you finish, the more stars: ★★★ within 7 seconds for every tile of the tower, ★★ within twice that time, otherwise ★. The clock runs only while the level is on the screen.

Replay a level from the Levels map for more stars. Tokens come only the first time. The Stars leaderboard counts all your stars.

Quick tower and the Endless map

Quick tower opens after level 20: Easy, Medium, Hard, Random or Custom (size up to 10×10, difficulty, height, pairs or triples, colours).

The Endless map opens after level 50. Open the sectors around the ones you have, one by one. A locked sector comes back for free when the sectors around it are finished.

On the map you have missions: finish sectors, lines and squares of sectors. Each step pays once.

Tower of the day and Day streak

A new tower every day, the same for everyone: easier on Monday, harder at the weekend. Clear it completely for +2 Tokens.

Clear it every day from Monday to Sunday for +50 Tokens. Missed a day? Tap it in the calendar and bring it back for 5 Tokens or an ad, up to 3 days a week. On Monday you can still bring back last week’s days.

Tokens

You earn Tokens for levels, towers, sectors, missions and Tower of the day. A few also come back on their own: +2 every 4 hours, up to 15.

Use them for one more attempt, hints, Undo or new themes. Tokens never expire.

Themes and settings

Themes change the tiles, the map and the music. Your progress stays the same.

In Settings you can choose the language, the background (light, dark or like your phone), the sound, the music and the vibration.
