Katalumo help

Rules, tips and answers for Katalumo.

How to play

Place every tile so that each of its edges has the same colour as the edge it touches. Edges on the border of the board are plain, so tiles with a plain edge go on the border.

Tap a tile in your hand, then tap an empty space. Turn a tile with ↻ or by tapping it again. ♻ Hand brings other tiles from the deck.

A tile can fit a space and still be in the wrong place: every board has exactly one solution. A full frame, row or column lights up only when every tile in it is right.

Attempts

End attempt checks your board: right tiles stay for good, wrong ones come off. Each attempt you end uses one of your attempts.

When the attempts run out, the board locks. Watch an ad, unlock it with Tokens, or start again for free: ↺ Restart level or ↺ Restart board.

Levels 1 to 4 never lock: when the attempts run out, you get a free one to keep going.

Help with Tokens

Add a tile puts one right tile in its place for 5 Tokens.

Solve places all the missing tiles. The price depends on how many spaces are still empty.

Neither changes your stars: only your time counts.

Levels and stars

Play the levels in order. Every level is the same for everyone. The first ones are small and easy, every 5th level is Hard, every 25th Very hard. The biggest board in the levels is 12×12.

The faster you finish, the more stars: ★★★ within 7 seconds for every empty space, ★★ within twice that time, otherwise ★. The clock runs only while the level is on the screen. You can leave a level at any time: it waits for you.

You get Tokens for a level only the first time you finish it. Replay any level from the Levels map to earn more stars. The Stars leaderboard counts all your stars.

Quick board and the Endless map

Quick board opens after level 20: Easy, Medium, Hard, Random or Custom (any size up to 30×30, your colours). Easy and Medium boards pay 1 Token.

The Endless map opens after level 50. Open the sectors next to the ones you have, one by one. A locked sector opens again for free when the sectors around it are finished.

On the map you have missions: finish sectors, lines and squares of sectors. Each step pays once.

Board of the day and Day streak

A new 8×8 board every day, the same for everyone: easier at the start of the week, harder at the weekend. You get 7 attempts. Solve it completely for +2 Tokens.

Solve it every day from Monday to Sunday for +50 Tokens. Missed a day? Tap it in the calendar and bring it back for 5 Tokens or an ad, up to 3 days a week. On Monday you can still bring back last week’s days.

Turn on the Daily reminder in Settings to get a note when the new board arrives.

Days follow the real calendar. If the phone’s date is changed by hand, boards pay no Tokens and the day is not marked.

Tokens and the shop

You start with 15 Tokens. You earn more for levels, Quick boards, sectors, missions and Board of the day. When you have fewer than 15, +2 come back every 4 hours.

Use them to unlock a board, for Add a tile and Solve, or for new themes. Tokens never expire.

In the Shop you can buy Tokens and theme packs. Restore purchases brings back what you bought, for example on a new phone. Got a code? Use Redeem a code in Settings.

Your progress

Katalumo works offline. The shop and ads need the internet.

Sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center with iCloud (iPhone), and your progress is saved in the cloud and comes back on a new phone.

Themes and settings

Themes change the tiles, the map and the music. Your progress stays the same. Seasonal themes arrive often.

In Settings you can choose the language, the background (light, dark or like your phone), the sound, the music and the vibration, turn the daily reminder and news on or off, and play the tutorial again.
