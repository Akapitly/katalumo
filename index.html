Play the demo

The demo is a 3×3 area of sectors with every difficulty on show, from an easy board to the empty 💎 one. No timer, no ads, no sign-in. Your progress stays in this browser.

Match the edges

Every tile has four coloured edges. A tile fits only where each edge matches the tile it touches; the sector border is blank. Rotate with ⟳ and tap a cell to place.

One true place

A tile can match its neighbours and still belong elsewhere. When an attempt ends, only tiles in their true place stay. Fill a sector to earn ★ points and Tokens.

An endless map

In the full game the map never ends: open sectors next to finished ones, climb three mission ladders, unlock locked sectors by surrounding them, and switch between the Flowers and Space themes.
