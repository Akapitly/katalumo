Katalumo

Endless tile puzzle

Every tile has four coloured edges. Find the spot where each edge meets its match, fill the whole sector, and the map opens up. Then there's the next one, and the map goes on as far as you do.

  • An infinite map where you choose your own path
  • Single boards: 8×8, 10×10, 12×12 and bigger
  • Map of the day and Board of the day, the same puzzle for everyone
  • Missions, leaderboards and achievements
  • Three themes, Space, Flowers and Jewels, each with its own soundtrack
  • Light and dark mode

Coming soon to Android and iPhone

Katalumo Twist

Tile match

Take matching tiles off the pyramid. Tap a free tile, then find its twin: each tap turns a tile a quarter, and when both lie the same way, the pair comes off. Clear a sector layer by layer, then open the next one.

  • An endless map of 6×6, 8×8 or 10×10 sectors
  • Single towers: low, tall or very tall
  • Pairs or triples, two or three matching tiles at a time
  • Map of the day and Tower of the day, the same for every player
  • Missions, hints and shuffles to keep you moving
  • Three themes: Flowers, Space and Jewels

Coming soon to Android and iPhone
