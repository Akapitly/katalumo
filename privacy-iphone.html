Privacy policy for the iPhone and iPad apps

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad apps of the Katalumo family, Katalumo, Katalumo Twist and Katalumo Pattern Diary, from the App Store. Below, “the game” means any of them; they handle data in the same way, and where a game differs, this policy says so. The games are made by A. Rowicka, an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described here (data controller). Contact: info@katalumo.com.

The short version

  • Your progress, points, Tokens and themes, and in Katalumo Pattern Diary your diaries, pages and notes, are stored on your device.
  • The game shows ads only when you choose to watch one, and asks for your permission before any personalised advertising. Katalumo Pattern Diary shows non-personalised ads only.
  • Payments are handled by the App Store. We never see your card details.
  • Leaderboards and achievements (Katalumo and Katalumo Twist) are optional and run on Apple Game Center.
  • We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Your maps, boards, settings, points and Tokens are saved locally on your device (in app storage). They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Pattern Diary: diaries, photos and images

In Katalumo Pattern Diary, your diaries, pages, notes and cover photos are saved only on your device. A cover photo is used only if you choose one yourself in the system photo picker; the game sees only that photo. Saving a page to your photo library happens only when you tap Save image: the app asks for permission to add photos and never reads your library. Sharing a page happens only when you tap Share and uses the system share sheet, so you decide where the image goes. If iCloud Backup is turned on, your device backup may include the game's data under your Apple Account settings; we have no access to it.

Ads

The game offers one kind of ad: a rewarded video you can choose to watch for a reward in the game, for example one extra attempt in a locked sector, a few hints (in Katalumo Twist), keeping your day streak, or a Weekend page in Katalumo Pattern Diary. Ads are never shown without your tap, and they are limited to those suitable for teens and adults. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your IP address, device and app information, your interaction with the ad and, only with your permission, your device's advertising identifier.

Your permission. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game first asks for your consent using Google's consent tool. If you agree to personalised ads there, Apple's own tracking request follows (“Allow … to track your activity”). Elsewhere, Apple's tracking request appears when you first open the game. The advertising identifier is used, and ads are personalised, only if you choose “Allow”. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, or decline in the consent tool, the game requests only non-personalised ads. Katalumo Pattern Diary always requests non-personalised ads only and does not track you: it shows Google's consent tool where it applies, never asks for Apple's tracking permission and never uses the advertising identifier.

Changing your mind. You can change your choices at any time: in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices (where the consent tool applies), and in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Token packs and theme packs (for example the Christmas Pack) are sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The game only receives confirmation of the purchase (product, transaction number) so it can add the Tokens or the themes. To show which theme packs you own, for example after “Restore purchases” on a new device, the game asks the App Store for the purchases of your Apple Account. In Katalumo Pattern Diary, packs of colours, shapes and papers, special diaries and the Club are one-time purchases sold through the App Store in the same way and can be restored the same way. We never receive your card or payment details.

Leaderboards and achievements

Katalumo and Katalumo Twist use Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements; Katalumo Pattern Diary has none. When you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname and your scores and achievements are sent to Apple and shown to other players. You control Game Center in your device settings (Settings → Game Center). See Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Daily reminder

Katalumo and Katalumo Twist offer a daily reminder; Katalumo Pattern Diary has none. If you turn on the daily reminder in the game, the game schedules one notification a day on your device. The reminder is created on the device itself; no data is sent for it. You can turn it off at any time in the game's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Play statistics

We see the aggregated, anonymous figures that Apple and Google AdMob provide to every developer (for example, the number of downloads or ads watched). Katalumo Pattern Diary (from its first version), Katalumo Twist (from version 1.1.0) and Katalumo (from version 1.0.1) also use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and to balance it. It records anonymous game events, for example that a sector was finished or locked, which kind of help was used, that a purchase was made, that a daily challenge was completed, or that a diary page was finished or an image shared, together with basic technical data (app version, device model, operating system, country derived from the IP address, and a random app-instance identifier). The advertising identifier is not collected for this, the data is not used for advertising or tracking, and statistics are kept for at most 14 months and used only in aggregated form.

Who receives data, and for how long

The game shares data only with the companies named in this policy, Google (ads, statistics) and Apple (purchases, Game Center), and only for the purposes described here. Both protect your data to a standard equal to this policy, under their own privacy policies and the data-protection terms they provide to developers. We keep no user database of our own: the game has no accounts, and your game data stays on your device. Data held by Google or Apple is kept and deleted under their policies.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of data we hold about you, or object to its use: write to info@katalumo.com. To withdraw your consent to personalised ads, use the settings described under “Changing your mind”. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. Data processed by Google (ads) can also be managed in your Google account settings; data processed by Apple (App Store, Game Center) in your Apple Account settings.

Children

The Katalumo games are for teens and adults and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
