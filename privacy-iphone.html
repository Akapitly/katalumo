Privacy policy — iPhone apps

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the iPhone apps of the Katalumo family — Katalumo and Katalumo Twist — from the App Store. Below, “the game” means either of them; they handle data in the same way. The games are made by A. Rowicka, an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described here (data controller). Contact: info@katalumo.com.

The short version

  • Your progress, points and Tokens are stored on your device.
  • The game shows ads only when you choose to watch one, and asks for your permission before any personalised advertising.
  • Payments are handled by the App Store. We never see your card details.
  • Leaderboards and achievements are optional and run on Apple Game Center.
  • We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Your maps, boards, settings, points and Tokens are saved locally on your device (in app storage). They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads

The game offers one kind of ad: a rewarded video you can choose to watch in a locked sector to get one extra attempt (in Katalumo Twist, also to get a few hints). Ads are never shown without your tap, and they are limited to those rated for a general audience. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your IP address, device and app information, your interaction with the ad and — only with your permission — your device's advertising identifier.

Your permission. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game first asks for your consent using Google's consent tool. If you agree to personalised ads there, Apple's own tracking request follows (“Allow … to track your activity”). Elsewhere, Apple's tracking request appears when you first open the game. The advertising identifier is used, and ads are personalised, only if you choose “Allow”. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, or decline in the consent tool, the game requests only non-personalised ads.

Changing your mind. You can change your choices at any time: in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices (where the consent tool applies), and in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Token packs are sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The game only receives confirmation of the purchase (product, transaction number) so it can add the Tokens. We never receive your card or payment details.

Leaderboards and achievements

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center. When you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname and your scores and achievements are sent to Apple and shown to other players. You control Game Center in your device settings (Settings → Game Center). See Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Play statistics

The game sends us no play statistics. We see only the aggregated, anonymous figures that Apple and Google AdMob provide to every developer (for example, the number of downloads or ads watched).

Who receives data, and for how long

The game shares data only with the companies named in this policy — Google (ads) and Apple (purchases, Game Center) — and only for the purposes described here. Both protect your data to a standard equal to this policy, under their own privacy policies and the data-protection terms they provide to developers. We keep no user database of our own: the game has no accounts, and your game data stays on your device. Data held by Google or Apple is kept and deleted under their policies.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of data we hold about you, or object to its use: write to info@katalumo.com. To withdraw your consent to personalised ads, use the settings described under “Changing your mind”. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. Data processed by Google (ads) can also be managed in your Google account settings; data processed by Apple (App Store, Game Center) in your Apple Account settings.

Children

The Katalumo games are puzzle games for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
