Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the games of the Katalumo family, Katalumo and Katalumo Twist, in all their versions: the Android apps on Google Play, the iPhone apps on the App Store and later versions for other platforms. Below, “the game” means any of them; they all handle data in the same way. The games are made by A. Rowicka, an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described here (data controller). Contact: info@katalumo.com.

The short version

  • Your progress, points and Tokens are stored on your device.
  • The Android and iPhone apps show ads only when you choose to watch one, and ask for your consent before any personalised advertising.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card details.
  • Leaderboards and achievements are optional and run on Google Play Games or Apple Game Center.
  • The Android apps send anonymous play statistics so we can balance the game. No advertising ID is used for them.
  • We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Your maps, boards, settings, points and Tokens are saved locally on your device (in the app's storage). They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Ads (Android and iPhone apps)

The mobile apps offer one kind of ad: a rewarded video you can choose to watch in a locked sector to get one extra attempt (in Katalumo Twist, also to get a few hints). Ads are never shown without your tap. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and your interaction with the ad.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices. Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

On iPhone, the app also asks through Apple's own tracking request (“Allow … to track your activity”) before the advertising identifier is used. The request appears once: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland after you agree in Google's consent tool, elsewhere when you first open the game. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the game requests only non-personalised ads. You can change this choice at any time in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Ads are limited to those suitable for teens and adults.

Purchases (Android and iPhone apps)

Token packs are sold through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). Google or Apple processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The game only receives confirmation of the purchase (product, order token) so it can add the Tokens. We never receive your card or payment details.

Leaderboards and achievements

Leaderboards and achievements use Google Play Games. When you are signed in to Play Games, your Play Games profile name and your scores and achievements are sent to Google and shown to other players on the leaderboards. Your Play Games profile and visibility are controlled in the Google Play Games settings on your device. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

On iPhone, leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center. When you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname and your scores and achievements are sent to Apple and shown to other players. You control Game Center in your device settings (Settings → Game Center). See Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Play statistics (Android apps)

To understand how the game is played and to balance it, the Android apps use Google Analytics for Firebase. It records anonymous game events, for example that a sector was finished or locked, which kind of help was used, that a Token pack was bought or that a daily challenge was completed, together with basic technical data (app version, device model, operating system, country derived from the IP address, and a random app-instance identifier). The advertising identifier is not collected for this, and the data is not used for advertising. Statistics are kept for a limited time (at most 14 months) and are used only in aggregated form.

Who receives data, and for how long

The game shares data only with the companies named in this policy, Google and Apple, and only for the purposes described here. Both protect your data to a standard equal to this policy, under their own privacy policies and the data-protection terms they provide to developers. We keep no user database of our own: the game has no accounts, and your game data stays on your device. Data held by Google or Apple is kept and deleted under their policies.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of data we hold about you, or object to its use: write to info@katalumo.com. To withdraw your consent to personalised ads, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. Data processed by Google (ads, Google Play, Play Games, Firebase) can also be managed in your Google account settings; data processed by Apple (App Store, Game Center) in your Apple Account settings.

Children

The Katalumo games are puzzle games for teens and adults and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
