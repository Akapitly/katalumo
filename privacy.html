Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the games of the Katalumo family, Katalumo, Katalumo Twist and Katalumo Pattern Diary, as Android apps on Google Play and in later versions for computers. Below, “the game” means any of them; they handle data in the same way, and where a game differs, this policy says so. The games are made by A. Rowicka, an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described here (data controller). Contact: info@katalumo.com.

The short version

  • Your progress, points, Tokens, themes, diaries and drawings are stored on your device.
  • The game shows ads only when you choose to watch one, and ask for your consent before any personalised advertising.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • Leaderboards and achievements (in Katalumo and Katalumo Twist) are optional and run on Google Play Games. When you are signed in there, Katalumo also keeps a copy of your progress in your Play Games account, so it comes back on a new phone.
  • The game sends anonymous play statistics so we can balance the game and see which of our own ads bring players. No advertising ID is used for them.
  • We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Your maps, boards, settings, points and Tokens are saved locally on your device (in the app's storage). They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Katalumo: your progress in your Play Games account

From version 1.1.5, when you are signed in to Google Play Games, Katalumo saves a copy of your progress (map, boards, Tokens, day streak and calendar, missions, themes and packs you own) as a saved game in your Play Games account, so that it comes back when you change or reinstall your phone. The copy is stored by Google in your account; we cannot see it. If the copy and the phone differ, the game keeps the one with more progress. You can delete it in your Google Play Games settings. Without Play Games your progress stays only on your device.

Pattern Diary: diaries, photos and images

In Katalumo Pattern Diary, your diaries, pages, notes and cover photos are saved only on your device. A cover photo is used only if you choose one yourself in the system photo picker; the game sees only that photo. Saving a page to your gallery or sharing it happens only when you tap Save or Share, and sharing uses your device's share menu, so you decide where the image goes. If your device's backup is turned on, Android may include the game's data in your Google backup under your Google account settings; we have no access to it.

Backup file. In Katalumo Pattern Diary, Settings → Back up your diaries saves one file with your diaries, pages and notes in a place you choose (for example Downloads or Google Drive). The file is made on your device and goes only where you save it; we never receive it. Settings → Restore from a backup reads a backup file you pick and adds its diaries to the game. Purchases are not in the file; they come back with Restore purchases.

Ads

The mobile apps offer one kind of ad: a rewarded video you can choose to watch for a reward in the game, for example one extra attempt in a locked sector, a few hints (in Katalumo Twist) keeping your day streak, or an extra Weekend page in Katalumo Pattern Diary. Ads are never shown without your tap. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and your interaction with the ad.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices. Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads. Katalumo Pattern Diary always requests non-personalised ads only. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads are limited to those suitable for teens and adults.

Purchases

Token packs and theme packs (for example the Christmas Pack) are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The game only receives confirmation of the purchase (product, order token) so it can add the Tokens or the themes. To show which theme packs you own, for example after “Restore purchases” on a new device, the game asks the store for the purchases of your account. In Katalumo Pattern Diary, packs of colours, shapes and papers, special diaries and the Club are one-time purchases sold through Google Play in the same way and can be restored the same way. We never receive your card or payment details.

Leaderboards and achievements

Katalumo and Katalumo Twist use Google Play Games for leaderboards and achievements; Katalumo Pattern Diary has none. When you are signed in to Play Games, your Play Games profile name and your scores and achievements are sent to Google and shown to other players on the leaderboards. Your Play Games profile and visibility are controlled in the Google Play Games settings on your device. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder in the game, the game schedules one notification a day on your device. The reminder is created on the device itself; no data is sent for it. You can turn it off at any time in the game's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Play statistics

To understand how the game is played and to balance it, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records anonymous game events, for example that a sector was finished or locked, which kind of help was used, that a Token pack was bought that a daily challenge was completed, or that a diary page was finished or an image shared, together with basic technical data (app version, device model, operating system, country derived from the IP address, and a random app-instance identifier). The advertising identifier is not collected for this, and the data is not used to personalise ads. Measuring our ads (Katalumo, from version 1.1.5). We advertise the games with Google Ads. To learn whether an install or an action in the game came from one of our ads, Google Analytics for Firebase may link the install to the Google ad that led to it (through the Google Play install referrer) and share these events with Google Ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland this happens only if you agreed to it in Google's consent message; you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Statistics are kept for a limited time (at most 14 months) and are used only in aggregated form.

Links into the game

Links on katalumo.com (for example katalumo.com/k/shop) and in our posts can open a place in Katalumo, such as the shop or today's puzzle. The game records in the play statistics that a link opened it and which kind of link it was (for example a link from a post), nothing else about where you came from.

News notifications

Katalumo Pattern Diary can send a notification when a new special diary or a Weekend page arrives, and only if you agree: the game asks first, then your device asks for permission. If you agree, Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) gives your device a random notification token and the game subscribes it to the Pattern Diary news topic. The token is not linked to your name or to an account. You can turn notifications off at any time in the game (Settings → Notifications), which deletes the token, or in your device's notification settings. Tapping a notification may open a place in the game, for example a special diary.

Katalumo (from version 1.1.5) works the same way, with its own topics: if you allow notifications on your device (for example for the daily reminder), the game can receive messages about the game itself; news and offers (new themes, events, sales) come only if you also say yes to them in the game. You can turn news and offers off at any time in the game (Settings → News and offers), and all notifications in your device's notification settings.

Game settings and error reports

Katalumo Pattern Diary uses Firebase Remote Config (Google) to receive game settings, for example the end of a special diary's sale, the size of the free version, the days of the Weekend page or a short message on the start screen. For this the app sends a random installation identifier, the app version and basic device information. The app also uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to receive a report when it crashes or when the game meets an error. A report contains technical details of the error, the app version, device model, operating system and a random installation identifier, and nothing from your diaries. Google keeps these reports for 90 days. Katalumo (from version 1.1.5) uses the same two services: Remote Config for game settings such as rewards and prices in Tokens, the season of the Christmas themes, the minimum app version and a short message on the start screen, and Crashlytics for crash and error reports, which contain nothing from your maps or boards.

Who receives data, and for how long

The game shares data only with the company named in this policy, Google, and only for the purposes described here. Google protects your data to a standard equal to this policy, under its own privacy policy and the data-protection terms it provides to developers. We keep no user database of our own: the game has no accounts, and your game data stays on your device. Data held by Google is kept and deleted under its policies.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of data we hold about you, or object to its use: write to info@katalumo.com. To withdraw your consent to personalised ads, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. Data processed by Google (ads, Google Play, Play Games, Firebase) can also be managed in your Google account settings.

Children

The Katalumo games are puzzle and creative games for teens and adults and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
